Q: Tell me about RunWay.

A: RunWay is a fashion e-commerce platform built during Harsh's Cognizant internship. It follows a microservices architecture with six services: Auth, Catalog, Order, Payment, Engagement, and API Gateway. Each service has its own MySQL database. Harsh was responsible for the Eureka Server, API Gateway, Catalog Service, and several Angular frontend modules.

---

Q: What was Harsh's biggest challenge in RunWay?

A: The biggest challenge was handling distributed transactions. Creating an order required coordination between Order, Catalog, and Payment services. The team solved this using the Saga Orchestration Pattern where the Order Service acted as the orchestrator and executed compensation actions if a failure occurred.

---

Q: Why did Harsh use Saga Pattern?

A: Traditional database transactions cannot span multiple independent microservice databases. Saga allows each service to perform a local transaction while maintaining overall consistency through compensating actions when failures occur.

---

Q: Why separate databases for each microservice?

A: Database-per-service improves service independence, scalability, fault isolation, and loose coupling. Each microservice owns its data and can evolve independently.

---

Q: Tell me about Harsh's Personal AI Chatbot.

A: It is a RAG-powered portfolio assistant built using Angular 21, FastAPI, ChromaDB, Sentence Transformers, and Groq. Recruiters can ask questions about Harsh's profile and receive evidence-backed answers. It also includes confidence gating, a JD matcher, JWT-protected admin dashboard, and human-in-the-loop learning.

---

Q: What is RAG?

A: Retrieval-Augmented Generation combines information retrieval with LLM generation. Instead of relying only on model memory, relevant information is first retrieved from a knowledge base and then supplied to the LLM, resulting in more accurate and grounded responses.

---

Q: Why build a chatbot instead of a portfolio website?

A: Traditional portfolios require recruiters to search manually. Harsh wanted recruiters to ask natural-language questions and instantly receive relevant answers. The chatbot acts as an interactive portfolio.

---

Q: What is confidence gating?

A: Confidence gating measures retrieval confidence before generating an answer. If the retrieval score falls below a threshold, the chatbot refuses to answer rather than hallucinating.

---

Q: Why did Harsh choose ChromaDB?

A: ChromaDB is lightweight, easy to integrate, open source, and well suited for RAG applications that require semantic retrieval and vector search.

---

Q: What is human-in-the-loop learning?

A: Unanswered questions are stored and reviewed later. Harsh manually provides answers, re-ingests them into the knowledge base, and improve the chatbot's future responses.

Scale:

The platform exposed more than 80 APIs across different services.

Biggest Technical Challenge:

The biggest challenge faced by the team was maintaining consistency across multiple services during distributed transactions.

For example:
An order could involve inventory updates, order creation, and payment processing across separate databases.

Solution:

The team implemented the Saga Orchestration Pattern.

The Order Service acted as the orchestrator and coordinated actions across Catalog and Payment services.

If any step failed, compensating actions were executed automatically to restore consistency.

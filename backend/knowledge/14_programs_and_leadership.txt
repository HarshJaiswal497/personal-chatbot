# Programs and Leadership Development

## McKinsey Forward Program

Organization:
McKinsey & Company

Duration:
October 2025 – December 2025

Program Type:
Global Professional Development Program

Selection:

Harsh was selected for the McKinsey Forward Program, a globally recognized learning initiative designed to develop professional and leadership skills among students and early-career professionals.

Overview:

The program focuses on helping participants build practical workplace skills that complement technical expertise.

Key Areas Covered:

### Problem Solving

* Breaking down complex problems
* Root cause analysis
* Structured thinking
* Decision making

### Communication

* Professional communication
* Stakeholder communication
* Presenting ideas clearly
* Storytelling using data

### Leadership

* Influencing without authority
* Team collaboration
* Accountability
* Growth mindset

### Adaptability

* Learning new skills quickly
* Managing uncertainty
* Handling changing priorities

### Personal Effectiveness

* Time management
* Productivity
* Career planning

Projects and Activities:

Throughout the program, Harsh worked on practical business scenarios and case-based exercises that simulated real-world workplace challenges.

Key Learnings:

* Structured problem solving
* Executive communication
* Leadership fundamentals
* Professional collaboration
* Business thinking

Impact:

The program significantly improved Harsh's communication, presentation, and structured thinking skills and complemented his technical background with business-oriented problem solving.
